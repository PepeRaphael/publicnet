Designed for True Musicians

From strict rhythmic constraints to advanced polyrhythms.

Universal MIDI Support

Plug-and-play compatibility. Whether you own a Yamaha Clavinova, a Roland, or a basic MIDI controller, our engine detects and evaluates your keystrokes with millisecond accuracy.

LilyPond Rendering Engine

Say goodbye to blurry, static PDFs. Every exercise is dynamically engraved using the industry-standard LilyPond engine, providing beautiful, vector-quality SVG sheet music on the fly.

Absolute Parametric Control

Customize everything. Set specific key signatures, restrict allowed note durations (from whole notes to sixteenths), cap chord intervals, and isolate specific keyboard ranges per hand.

Polyrhythms & Syncopation

Push your rhythmic boundaries. Toggle advanced settings to inject binary/ternary polyrhythms and complex syncopations into your sight-reading sessions.

High-Resolution Velocity

We read High-Res MIDI (CC 88) for extreme dynamic precision. The engine evaluates not just if you hit the right note, but exactly *how* you struck it (p, mf, f).

Smart Preset System

Save your favorite training configurations as JSON presets. Switch instantly from a "Chopin-style wide leaps" warmup to a "Bach-style counterpoint" session.

Meet the AI Learning Mode

Available exclusively in the Premium tier, the AI engine acts as your personal conservatoire professor. It analyzes your real-time performance and adapts the generated score instantly.

  • Adaptive Difficulty Scaling
  • Weakness Targeting (e.g., forces more ledger lines if you hesitate)
  • Instant Rhythm Correction Paths
  • Continuous Flow without UI interruption
